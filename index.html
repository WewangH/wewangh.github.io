Welcome

I am a PhD candidate in Accounting and Management at Harvard Business School, and I am on the 2026–27 academic job market.

My research examines firms’ internal information environment, particularly the mechanisms that carry local information upward from employees to decision makers in support of organizational learning and managerial decision-making. I am also interested in the role of artificial intelligence in management control systems and in organizations more broadly.

I combine large-sample archival analyses with surveys, field studies, and field experiments, both within a firm and in broad-sample settings, and I apply machine learning, natural language processing, and large language model tools to construct measures from novel textual data.

My job market paper, Echoing Employee Reviews, asks whether and how firms process the information in employee reviews, and what follows when they do.
